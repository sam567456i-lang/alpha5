# AlphaGrid Master Roadmap

This is the permanent macro roadmap for the entire AlphaGrid ecosystem. It tracks the chronological execution from raw data foundation all the way through to execution and machine learning. 

## Phase 0: Foundation
**Status: ✅ FROZEN**
The infrastructure, governance, determinism framework, replay engines, SDKs, and event buses.

## Phase 1: Platform 10 Completion
**Status: IN PROGRESS**
The creation of 24+ objective evidence capability families (Stage 1 through Stage 4).

## Phase 2: Platform 10 Integrated Certification
**Status: PLANNED**
Before Platform 11 expansion is permitted, Platform 10 must pass full-system certification:
- Zero duplicate concept ownership.
- Identical bit-for-bit replay checksums across the entire system.
- Strict SDK-only access from consumers.

## Phase 3: Platform 11 Methodology Completion
**Status: PLANNED**
The creation of subjective interpretation profiles that consume Platform 10 evidence.
- **Institutional:** ICT, SMC, Wyckoff, Auction Market Theory, Volume Profile.
- **Classical:** Price Action, Trend Following, Pattern Breakout.
- **Quantitative:** Mean Reversion, Momentum, Pullback, Volatility Breakout, Regression, Statistical Arbitrage.
- **User:** Custom Profile SDK for third-party methodologies.

## Phase 4: Platform 11 Certification
**Status: PLANNED**
Validation that all methodologies produce deterministic signals without direct market data dependencies (pure SDK consumption).

## Phase 5: Platform 12 Decision Support
**Status: PLANNED**
**Prerequisites:**
1. **Decision Feature Registry:** Every feature Platform 12 consumes.
2. **Decision Policy Registry:** Every deterministic decision policy.
3. **Conflict Registry:** Priority rules for multi-methodology signals.
4. **Voting Registry:** Consensus strategies.
5. **Decision Lifecycle:** `Candidate` → `Evaluated` → `Scored` → `Ranked` → `Approved` → `Rejected` → `Archived`.

## Phase 6: Platform 13 Execution Intelligence
**Status: PLANNED**
Converting approved decisions into live market orders.

## Phase 7: Platform 14 Learning & Analytics
**Status: PLANNED**
Consuming history to calibrate the pipeline.

## Phase 8: Platform 15 External Interfaces
**Status: PLANNED**
Dashboards, APIs, and UI platforms.
