# AlphaGrid Architecture Principles

This document serves as the absolute single source of truth for the core architectural principles of AlphaGrid. All other documentation, architecture decision records, and system specifications must adhere to and reference these principles.

## 1. Manual Trading Principle
AlphaGrid is fundamentally and permanently designed for Manual Trading. It does not execute trades, place orders, or connect to brokerage execution APIs within its core production pipeline.

## 2. Decision Support Principle
AlphaGrid is a Decision Support System (DSS). It exists to provide institutional-grade trading intelligence that assists a human discretionary trader in making better, mathematically rigorous trading decisions.

## 3. Human Approval Principle
The human trader always remains the final decision maker. Any recommendations, execution guidance, or risk assessments generated by AlphaGrid require explicit human review and approval before execution occurs outside of the system.

## 4. Platform Purity Principle
Each platform must maintain strict boundaries and adhere to its specific scope of intelligence delivery. 
- Recommendations are not orders. 
- Risk assessments are not execution authorizations.
- Execution guidance is not broker routing.

## 5. Legacy Preservation Principle
Completed architectural phases remain historically intact. Enhancements or modifications to frozen architectures must occur via formal, superseding snapshot updates rather than destructive overwrites, preserving the repository evolution record.
