# Future Capabilities (Long-Term Backlog)

Not every capability belongs on the active roadmap. This document captures high-value, long-term capabilities that will remain out of active execution until prioritized in a future ADR.

## Advanced Data & Analytics
- Options analytics and Greeks
- Macro factor models
- Alternative data ingestion (e.g., credit card flows)
- Satellite imagery analysis
- ESG metrics tracking
- Fundamental analysis pipelines
- Crypto on-chain analytics

## Advanced Intelligence
- News and NLP parsing
- Reinforcement learning agents (P14)
- General Artificial Intelligence integrations

*When any of these items are ready to be implemented, they must be formally drafted into an ADR and properly slotted into the Capability Matrix.*
